Privacy Policy

For the mobile game Tiny Parking
Last updated: Sep 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Tiny Parking ("the Game", "we", "us") collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. The Game is published by Diva Labs.

By installing or playing the Game, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us directly

The Game does not ask you to create an account, and we do not request your name, email address, phone number, or postal address. We do not collect this information.

Information collected automatically by advertising partners

The Game displays advertisements supplied by third-party advertising networks. To deliver and measure those ads, these networks may automatically collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID)
  • Device information such as model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, and screen size
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as country or city) may be derived
  • Information about your interaction with ads, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped
  • Basic diagnostic information, such as crash reports and performance data, used to keep the Game working correctly

We do not receive your precise GPS location, and the Game does not request location permission.

2. How the information is used

Information collected through the Game is used only to:

  • Display advertisements, which is how the Game is funded
  • Measure whether ads were delivered correctly and prevent advertising fraud
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems so the Game can be improved

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to build profiles for purposes unrelated to the Game.

4. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and may be played by children as well as adults. We take the privacy of children seriously and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation as it applies to children, and Google Play's Families policy.

To protect younger players:

  • When you first open the Game, you are asked for your date of birth. This is used only to determine which advertising settings apply, and it is stored on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party.
  • Players identified as children, and players whose age is unknown, are shown only non-personalized, child-appropriate advertisements served exclusively through advertising networks that are certified under Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. Their advertising identifier is not used for interest-based advertising or remarketing.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not transmit persistent identifiers from children for advertising purposes.

5. How long information is kept

We do not operate servers and do not store your information ourselves. Any information collected by our advertising partners is retained according to their own retention policies, which are described in the privacy policies linked in section 3.

6. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.
  • Turn off personalized ads. The same Android settings screen lets you opt out of interest-based advertising across apps.
  • Stop all collection. Uninstalling the Game ends any further collection of information through it.
